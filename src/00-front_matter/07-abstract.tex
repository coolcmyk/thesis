This study evaluates instruction-conditioned UAV navigation through both mission completion and flight-intent adherence. UAV-Flow-Sim demonstrations will be used to LoRA-SFT OpenVLA-7B and $\pi_0$-FAST. For each model, M0 is the reference with a calibrated UAV interface, M1 is the SFT policy, and M2 continues M1 through GRPO; a continued-SFT control also starts from M1. GRPO rollouts and evaluation will use CoSyS-AirSim AirSimNH with primitive maneuvers, landmark interactions, and multi-step compositions. A simulator-based evaluator scores intents such as approaching, passing a specified side, orbiting, and holding position. Policies receive front RGB images, instructions, UAV state, and verified progress counts. Paired test episodes compare M2 with M1 and the continued-SFT control on mission success, intent adherence, collisions, safety interventions, efficiency, and training cost. Held-out combinations of trained maneuvers measure compositional generalization. Current evidence is limited to development checks; the UAV integration of $\pi_0$-FAST and the main experiments remain unfinished. The benefit of GRPO is therefore not yet established.

    \textbf{Keywords:} VLA, OpenVLA, $\pi_0$-FAST, UAV, LoRA, SFT, GRPO.
